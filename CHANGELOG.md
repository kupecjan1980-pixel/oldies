# Changes

## Since 0.1.0 (in the source)

- Ace Ventura (Windows 3.1, through Wine and OTVDM): `ace-ventura.sh` and `oldies-view`, with the dithering
  taken out and the picture upscaled; subtitles in English, German and Czech made from your own copy of the
  game (`tools/`), shown in time with the speech.

## 0.1.0

- The first release: Body Blows and Metal Mutant (DOS) in DOSBox Pure, with controllers, a pause menu in
  English, Czech and Slovak, save and load anywhere, upscaling (FSR 1, neural nets) to Full HD or the
  screen, frames in between, Metal Mutant's game language.
